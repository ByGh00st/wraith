# Community conduct

Keep collaboration respectful, specific and useful. Critique code and ideas without attacking people. Welcome questions, explain disagreements and respect different levels of experience.

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, doxxing, unwanted sexual content and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable in project spaces. Do not post credentials or sensitive security findings in public discussions.

Maintainers may moderate content or restrict participation to protect contributors and keep discussions productive. Address ordinary collaboration concerns to maintainers in the relevant issue or PR without reposting abusive or private material. For a sensitive concern, request a private contact channel without publishing its details. GitHub's own reporting tools are also available for platform abuse.

Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). This conduct policy is not a software-license amendment.
